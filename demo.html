This is the real app flow

What you just tried is what
the app does on every flight.

The demo above uses fixed sample data. In the app, the same three steps run on live weather and NOTAMs for the exact route, aircraft, and departure time you enter.

1

Enter your route, aircraft, and departure

Departure, destination, alternates, and planned departure time. Pick your aircraft from the library or build a profile around the one you fly, with your own personal minimums.

2

We pull the data and compute the numbers

Live METARs, TAFs, winds aloft, and AIRMETs; NOTAMs filtered to your window; weight and balance, takeoff and landing distances, and the full fuel plan from your aircraft's POH data.

3

Read the verdict, then verify the raw data

A scored GO, CAUTION, or NO-GO with an AI briefing in plain language. Every interpretation links back to the raw METAR, TAF, or NOTAM so you can check it yourself. You make the final call.
