Per the POH cruise table, up to about 189.6 KTAS at 10,000 ft on a standard day, burning roughly 22.7 GPH at that setting. Typical everyday cruise settings run slower and leaner — see the table above.

98 gallons usable. At a high-cruise burn of 22.7 GPH that is roughly 4.3 hours to dry tanks — plan with reserves, never to zero.
