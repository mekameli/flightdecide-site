Common Questions

Per the POH cruise table, up to about 107 KTAS at 6000 ft on a standard day, burning roughly 5.8 GPH at that setting. Typical everyday cruise settings run slower and leaner; see the table above.

22.5 gallons usable. At a high-cruise burn of 5.8 GPH that is roughly 3.9 hours to dry tanks. Plan with reserves, never to zero.

About 540 lb for a typically equipped example (1600 lb max gross minus a typical empty weight of 1060 lb). Your aircraft's actual empty weight and CG come from its own weight-and-balance records. Always use those, not type averages.

VNE (never exceed) is 141 kt and VNO (max structural cruising) is 107 kt. Full V-speed table above; verify against the POH for your serial.
