Common Questions

Per the POH cruise table, up to about 113 KTAS at 8000 ft on a standard day, burning roughly 8.9 GPH at that setting. Typical everyday cruise settings run slower and leaner; see the table above.

36 gallons usable. At a high-cruise burn of 8.9 GPH that is roughly 4.0 hours to dry tanks. Plan with reserves, never to zero.

About 900 lb for a typically equipped example (2150 lb max gross minus a typical empty weight of 1250 lb). Your aircraft's actual empty weight and CG come from its own weight-and-balance records. Always use those, not type averages.

VNE (never exceed) is 149 kt and VNO (max structural cruising) is 122 kt. Full V-speed table above; verify against the POH for your serial.
