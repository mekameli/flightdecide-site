Per the POH cruise table, up to about 153.3 KTAS at 8000 ft on a standard day, burning roughly 14.3 GPH at that setting. Typical everyday cruise settings run slower and leaner; see the table above.

87 gallons usable. At a high-cruise burn of 14.3 GPH that is roughly 6.1 hours to dry tanks. Plan with reserves, never to zero.
