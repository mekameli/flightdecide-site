Common Questions

Per the POH cruise table, up to about 153.3 KTAS at 8000 ft on a standard day, burning roughly 14.3 GPH at that setting. Typical everyday cruise settings run slower and leaner; see the table above.

87 gallons usable. At a high-cruise burn of 14.3 GPH that is roughly 6.1 hours to dry tanks. Plan with reserves, never to zero.

About 1450 lb for a typically equipped example (3600 lb max gross minus a typical empty weight of 2150 lb). Your aircraft's actual empty weight and CG come from its own weight-and-balance records. Always use those, not type averages.

VNE (never exceed) is 182 kt and VNO (max structural cruising) is 149 kt. Full V-speed table above; verify against the POH for your serial.
