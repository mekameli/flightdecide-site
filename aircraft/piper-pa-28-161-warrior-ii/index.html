Per the POH cruise table, up to about 120.6 KTAS at 8000 ft on a standard day, burning roughly 9.4 GPH at that setting. Typical everyday cruise settings run slower and leaner; see the table above.

48 gallons usable. At a high-cruise burn of 9.4 GPH that is roughly 5.1 hours to dry tanks. Plan with reserves, never to zero.
