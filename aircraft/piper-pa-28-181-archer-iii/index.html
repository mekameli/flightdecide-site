Takeoff distance

ASSOCIATED CONDITIONS: Power: FULL THROTTLE BEFORE BRAKE RELEASE. Air Conditioner: OFF. Runway: PAVED, LEVEL, & DRY. Airspeed: REFER TO TABLE AT RIGHT. Propeller: SENSENICH 76EM8S14-0-62. Flaps: UP. Takeoff speeds at 2,550 lb: LIFTOFF 60 KIAS, 50 FT 65 KIAS. Both sheets read at REF. LINE MAX. WEIGHT 2550 LBS and REF. LINE ZERO WIND, where the weight and wind panels are a straight horizontal line, so this is a single-panel read. Flaps up is the handbook's NORMAL TECHNIQUE (Section 4 accelerates to 60 KIAS, the liftoff speed printed on these two sheets); the 25 deg flaps sheets, Figures 5-9 and 5-13, are the SHORT FIELD, OBSTACLE CLEARANCE case and are recorded under also_read, not here.

Source: Pilot's Operating Handbook and FAA Approved Airplane Flight Manual, Piper PA-28-181 Archer III, Report VB-1611 (issued July 12, 1995; figures as revised through December 6, 1999), SN 2843001 and up · Takeoff

Landing distance

ASSOCIATED CONDITIONS: Power Off Approach, 40 deg Flaps, 66 KIAS, Full Stall Touchdown, Maximum Braking, Paved, Level, Dry Runway. (Figure 5-37, the ground-roll sheet, prints the same block without the 66 KIAS.) Both sheets read at REF. LINE MAX. WEIGHT 2,550 LBS and REF. LINE ZERO WIND. Rate of climb

ASSOCIATED CONDITIONS: Gross Weight: 2550 LBS. Power: FULL THROTTLE. Airspeed: 76 KIAS. Flaps: UP. Air Conditioner: OFF. Service Ceiling 14,085 FT, Absolute Ceiling 16,400 FT. The sheet's right-hand note divides the output scale at 300 FPM (Mixture Full Rich above it, Mixture Leaned 100 deg F Rich of Peak below it); every cell read here is above 300 FPM. This figure has no weight or wind panel at all -- 2550 lb is the chart's own stated condition.

Source: Pilot's Operating Handbook and FAA Approved Airplane Flight Manual, Piper PA-28-181 Archer III, Report VB-1611 (issued July 12, 1995; figures as revised through December 6, 1999), SN 2843001 and up · Climb

Common Questions

Per the POH cruise table, up to about 119 KTAS at 10,000 ft on a standard day, burning roughly 9.5 GPH at that setting. Typical everyday cruise settings run slower and leaner; see the table above.

48 gallons usable. At a high-cruise burn of 9.5 GPH that is roughly 5.1 hours to dry tanks. Plan with reserves, never to zero.

About 1000 lb for a typically equipped example (2550 lb max gross minus a typical empty weight of 1550 lb). Your aircraft's actual empty weight and CG come from its own weight-and-balance records. Always use those, not type averages.

VNE (never exceed) is 154 kt and VNO (max structural cruising) is 125 kt. Full V-speed table above; verify against the POH for your serial.
