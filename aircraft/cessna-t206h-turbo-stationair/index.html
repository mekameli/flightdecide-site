Common Questions

Per the POH cruise table, up to about 163 KTAS at 20,000 ft on a standard day, burning roughly 19 GPH at that setting. Typical everyday cruise settings run slower and leaner; see the table above.

87 gallons usable. At a high-cruise burn of 19 GPH that is roughly 4.6 hours to dry tanks. Plan with reserves, never to zero.

The handbook gives a maximum useful load of 1303 lb: its 3617 lb maximum ramp weight minus a standard empty weight of 2314 lb for a standard-equipped airplane. Ramp weight sits 17 lb above the 3600 lb maximum takeoff weight to allow for the fuel burned during start, taxi and runup. Your aircraft's actual empty weight and CG come from its own weight-and-balance records. Always use those, not handbook figures.

VNE (never exceed) is 182 kt and VNO (max structural cruising) is 149 kt. Full V-speed table above; verify against the POH for your serial.
