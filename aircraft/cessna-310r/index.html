Per the POH cruise table, up to about 205 KTAS at 10,000 ft on a standard day, burning roughly 29 GPH at that setting. Typical everyday cruise settings run slower and leaner; see the table above.

163 gallons usable. At a high-cruise burn of 29 GPH that is roughly 5.6 hours to dry tanks. Plan with reserves, never to zero.
