Common Questions

Per the POH cruise table, up to about 107 KTAS at 8000 ft on a standard day, burning roughly 6.1 GPH at that setting. Typical everyday cruise settings run slower and leaner; see the table above.

24.5 gallons usable. At a high-cruise burn of 6.1 GPH that is roughly 4.0 hours to dry tanks. Plan with reserves, never to zero.

About 590 lb for a typically equipped example (1670 lb max gross minus a typical empty weight of 1080 lb). Your aircraft's actual empty weight and CG come from its own weight-and-balance records. Always use those, not type averages.

VNE (never exceed) is 149 kt and VNO (max structural cruising) is 111 kt. Full V-speed table above; verify against the POH for your serial.
