How FlightDecide Helps

Every pilot asks the same question
before every flight. We help you answer it.

Built for private pilots who want more than a gut check. FlightDecide pulls together weather, performance, fuel, and weight data into one scored verdict, so you can launch informed or stay on the ground for the right reasons.

Route Weather, Window-Aware

Departure, destination, and up to four enroute TAF checkpoints along your route. Each forecast is interpreted for your planned takeoff and arrival window: VFR, MVFR, IFR, or LIFR, with severe-weather flags called out. Times are shown in plain UTC, and the raw TAF is one tap away.

Enroute Picture in One Place

Winds aloft, freezing levels, G-AIRMETs, SIGMETs, and PIREPs along your route. When coverage is missing for an area, FlightDecide tells you so. You never see a silent "all clear" that came from no data.

One Fuel Plan, Fully Worked

Climb, cruise, and descent fuel computed from your aircraft's POH data, then legal reserve added on top. You see fuel on board, fuel required, reserve minutes available, and whether you meet the rule for your operation. VFR or IFR reserve rules under Part 91.

Performance and Weight & Balance

Takeoff and landing distances with margin against the available runway. Max gross, ramp, and zero-fuel weights checked. CG plotted inside the envelope. Adjust passengers, bags, and fuel and watch the numbers move before you load the airplane.

NOTAMs that Matter for Your Window

Each NOTAM gets a plain-English summary and a window-impact line: in effect during your flight, starts after you land, or ended before takeoff. Raw NOTAM text stays available for verification. When NOTAM coverage is incomplete, the gap is called out instead of hidden.

Your Aircraft, Your Minimums

Build a profile around the aircraft you actually fly and the personal weather and fuel minimums you actually hold to. Every analysis is computed against your weights, arms, fuel arm, runway preferences, and weather floors. Not someone else's defaults.

Corrective Actions and Path to GO

Every analysis lists ranked Required and Recommended steps with specific numbers: exact lbs to shift, altitude to request, fuel to add. For CAUTION and NO GO flights, Path to GO highlights the smallest set of changes that would flip the verdict.

Optimal Cruise Altitude

Winds aloft scored at every candidate cruise altitude for winds, icing, turbulence, and ceiling clearance, then constrained to the hemispheric rule for your direction of flight. FlightDecide tells you which altitude gives the best ride and why.

Free E6B Calculator Suite

Ten flight-planning calculators built into a Tools tab: crosswind, density altitude, true airspeed, time-speed-distance, fuel and endurance, the wind triangle, cross-country planning, weight and balance, descent and glide, and holding entries. Free on every plan, including the free tier, and they work offline. The same tools also run in your browser at flightdecide.com/tools.

Simple by design

A clear flight picture
in three steps.

No juggling six apps to assemble the picture yourself. FlightDecide pulls it together and scores it, then you verify against the raw data and your official briefing.

Enter your route, aircraft, and departure

Set departure, destination, alternates, and your planned departure time. Choose your aircraft from the built-in library or build a profile around the one you actually fly. Add passengers, bags, and fuel load.

We pull weather, NOTAMs, and compute the numbers

Live METARs, TAFs along the route, winds aloft, freezing levels, G-AIRMETs, SIGMETs, and PIREPs. NOTAMs filtered by your flight window. Weight and balance, takeoff and landing distances, and the full fuel plan computed from digitized POH data for your aircraft type. Weather comes live from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov), and NOTAMs from FAA data, at the moment you run the analysis. Each scored category produces ranked corrective actions you can act on.

Read the verdict, scan the briefing, verify the raw data

Eight categories scored green, amber, or red. An AI briefing in plain language explains the risk and points out the items worth a second look. Every interpretation is backed by the raw METAR, TAF, or NOTAM so you can verify it yourself. You make the final call.

The Flight Profile

Your whole flight,
in one picture.

Terrain, winds aloft, icing and turbulence bands, ceilings, and the freezing level for KSEA to KPDX, drawn against your climb, cruise, and descent. The picture you'd normally stitch together across a handful of tools is already here, on one screen.

  • Every phase timed out: climb 12 min to 6,500 ft, cruise 42 min at 112 kt GS, descent 12 min.
  • Per-segment risks called out along the route, like Icing (MOD) 46-52 NM and terrain within 2,000 ft.
  • Drag along the route to read the numbers at any point. Toggle terrain, winds, ceilings, and the freezing level.
