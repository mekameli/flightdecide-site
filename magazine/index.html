Questions

Before you buy

Is this the FlightDecide app?

No. The FlightDecide app is our flight decision tool on the App Store. FlightDecide Magazine is a separate PDF magazine from the same team, sold through our own shop.

What do I get?

A PDF of about 20 pages; Issue 01 has 21 pages and is 13 MB. The pages are 8 by 10 inches: easy to read on a tablet, and they print on US Letter. On a phone, zoom in to read.

How do members get each issue?

On release day the new issue is posted in the members area of our shop with the PDF attached, and members get a notice that it is up.

When do new issues come out?

When each issue is ready, not on a fixed monthly date. Issue 02 is planned for early December, and new lesson sheets come out between issues.

What happens if I cancel?

The members area closes at the end of your current month or year. PDFs you already downloaded are yours to keep.

Is it the same as your free explainers?

Partly, and we say so. Each lesson starts from one of our explainers or reels, then gets more pages, charts and photos.

Can I share it?

Each copy is for the person who bought it. Run a flight school or a club? Email us about a license for your students.

Does it replace my training?

No. It is for learning and review. Your aircraft's handbook and your instructor come first.

Anything else?

Email support@flightdecide.com and a person from the FlightDecide team will answer.
