Questions

Before you buy

What do I get?

A PDF of about 20 pages. The pages are 8 by 10 inches: easy to read on a tablet, and they print on US Letter. On a phone, zoom in to read. It downloads right after checkout.

How do members get each issue?

On release day the new issue is posted in the members area of our shop with the PDF attached, and members get a notice that it is up.

When do new issues come out?

When each issue is ready, not on a fixed monthly date. Issue 02 is planned for early December. Members get each issue the day it is out, plus lesson sheets between issues, and single issues go on sale a week later.

What happens if I cancel?

The members area closes at the end of the period you paid for. PDFs you already downloaded are yours to keep.

Is it the same as your free explainers?

Partly, and we say so. Each lesson starts from one of our explainers or reels, then gets more pages, charts and photos. Hangar talk and the quiz are new in every issue.

Can I share it?

Each copy is for the person who bought it. Run a flight school or a club? Email us about a license for your students.

Does it replace my training?

No. It is for learning and review. Your aircraft's handbook and your instructor come first.

Anything else?

Email support@flightdecide.com and a person from the FlightDecide team will answer.
