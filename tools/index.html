Free Pilot Tools & Preflight Planning Calculators

An online E6B flight computer and preflight planning suite: the calculators and worksheets for the numbers every flight-planning and go/no-go decision runs on. No sign-up, no download. They work right in your browser.

Home › Free Pilot Tools

A free online E6B flight computer for preflight planning: these tools require no sign-up and run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded anywhere. They cover the flight-planning calculations pilots have run on E6-B flight computers for decades, from crosswind components and density altitude to the wind triangle, cross-country nav logs, and weight and balance. Built and maintained by the FlightDecide team, the same people who build the go/no-go decision-support app, each one comes with the formula, a worked example, and the judgment context you need to actually use the number. Every calculator here is also built into the FlightDecide app, in a Tools tab that works offline and is free on every plan, including the free tier, with no subscription needed.

Tools

Crosswind Calculator

Headwind and crosswind components from runway, wind, and gusts, including the gust component that matters in the flare.

Density Altitude Calculator

Pressure altitude and density altitude from field elevation, altimeter, and temperature. See what altitude your airplane thinks it's at.

True Airspeed Calculator

CAS to TAS, Mach, and density altitude from altitude and temperature. The speed you file and the input the wind triangle needs.

Time-Speed-Distance Calculator

Solve time, groundspeed, or distance from the other two, plus an ETA. The E6-B function pilots use on every leg.

Fuel Burn & Endurance Calculator

Gallons burned, endurance to dry tanks, and your margin over the legal reserve at landing.

Heading & Groundspeed Calculator

The wind triangle solved: wind correction angle, true and magnetic heading, and the groundspeed your leg times run on.

Cross-Country Trip Planner

The whole nav log: heading, groundspeed, leg time, ETA, and fuel for every leg, plus trip totals.

Weight & Balance Calculator

Gross weight, total moment, and CG from your loading, checked against your limits, with a weight-shift solver.

Descent & Glide Calculator

When to start down and what rate to fly (the 3° rule), plus your still-air glide range from any altitude.

Holding Pattern Entry

Direct, teardrop, or parallel, with the AIM sectors drawn around your heading and what to fly next.

Personal Minimums Worksheet

A printable, fill-in worksheet for setting your own ceiling, visibility, wind, and performance numbers on a calm day, long before a tempting forecast.
