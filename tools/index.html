One tool that checks all of it

FlightDecide runs winds, ceilings, visibility, NOTAMs, fuel, performance, and W&B for your specific flight window and aircraft, then scores it GO, CAUTION, or NO GO and shows the raw data behind every call.

Get FlightDecide on the App Store

Every calculator runs in your browser with nothing uploaded, and each comes with the formula, a worked example, and the judgment to use the number. They're built by the FlightDecide team, and built into the app's Tools tab too, where they work offline and stay free on every plan.

These tools are educational aids for flight-planning practice. They are advisory only and not a substitute for your POH, an official weather briefing, or your own judgment as pilot in command (14 CFR 91.3). Sources are cited on each tool's page. Last reviewed: August 26, 2026.
