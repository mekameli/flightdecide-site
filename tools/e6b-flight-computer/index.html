Online E6B Flight Computer

The E6B's two sides, taken apart: every job the wind side and the computer side do, as a calculator that shows its working.

Home › Free Pilot Tools › Online E6B

An E6B is a circular slide rule with a wind grid on the back. The wind side solves the wind triangle: slide the grid to your true airspeed, mark the wind, and read the correction angle and groundspeed. The computer side is a slide rule for anything that is a rate times a time (distance, fuel, climb, descent), with small windows for true airspeed and density altitude. Why the calculators show the formula

A paper E6B makes you do the arithmetic, which is how you notice that a 25-knot quartering headwind at 110 knots TAS costs you 14 knots of groundspeed. These give the answer at once, then show the formula and a worked example on the same page, so the check-your-work habit survives. The nav log generator runs the whole chain for every leg of a trip, and the tools page has the rest, from weight and balance to the METAR decoder.

The E6B answers; the briefing decides

FlightDecide runs the same numbers for your aircraft and flight window, alongside the weather, NOTAMs, fuel and weight and balance, and shows where any of them crosses your own minimums.
