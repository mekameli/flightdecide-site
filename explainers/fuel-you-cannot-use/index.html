The fuel you cannot use

Usable fuel is less than your tanks hold.

Home › Explainers › The fuel you cannot use

Usable fuel is less than total fuel, and the difference is a published number rather than a rounding error.

What the picture shows

The labels on the illustration, and what each one is pointing at.

  • THIS IS TOTAL, NOT USABLEThe brimmed fuel surface where it meets the filler neck at the top of the cut-away tank bay.
  • TRAPPED BELOW THE PICKUPThe thin separate band of fuel lying in the floor corrugations beneath the fuel pickup.
  • TOTAL FUEL GETS YOU HEREThe translucent dimmed ghost aeroplane further along the track line.
  • PLAN FROM THIS FIGUREThe ruled line in the fuel table on the open flight manual page.

What this teaches

  • An aircraft's total fuel capacity includes an unusable quantity that cannot be relied on in flight.
  • Fuel planning uses the usable figure from the approved flight manual.

Where to read more
