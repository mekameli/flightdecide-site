What we teach

The lessons your line sits beside

Each reel takes one thing pilots use and opens it up: magnetos, carburetor ice, the fuel system of a high-wing trainer, the gyro instruments, a constant-speed prop, the coffin corner. Every claim is checked against FAA handbooks and the aircraft's own manual before it is published, and we publish five to seven reels a week.

Formats

What you can book

Reel package

One teaching reel with your sponsor line, posted as an Instagram reel, a Facebook reel and a YouTube Short.

Series

Four reel packages over a month around one theme, such as engines, weather or the traffic pattern. One series runs at a time.

Product X-ray

A reel built around your product's category, for example how active noise cancelling works inside a headset. Same fact-check, same teaching.

Every package includes

  • One spoken sponsor sentence after the lesson's payoff, and up to 5 seconds of your name on screen
  • Your name in the caption, and your link in the Facebook first comment and the YouTube Short's description
  • A 7-day results report from the platforms' own insights: views, reach, saves, shares and link clicks where they are reported
  • About two weeks from agreed terms to publishing; posts stay up, because we do not delete published lessons
  • Ad use of the reel and category exclusivity, on request

Our rules

What a sponsor controls, and what it does not

  • The teaching is not for sale.Sponsors cannot direct the lesson, its explanation, its conclusions or any safety point.
  • You approve every line that names your product.For a Product X-ray you also confirm the product facts we use. The explanation and its conclusions stay ours.
  • Every sponsored post is labelled.The Paid partnership label on Instagram and Facebook, Paid promotion on YouTube, and your name in the caption.
  • At most one reel in four.Our followers came for the lessons, and sponsors never crowd them out.
  • Only products we would recommend unpaid.Headsets, pilot supplies, simulators, insurance, fuel and engines, training. No competing flight apps.
  • Plain about how we make it.Our narrator is a synthetic voice, and our pictures are 3D renders and generated stills, disclosed where each platform asks.
